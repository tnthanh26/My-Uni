Information Collection and Use

To provide our services, the Application may collect information including:

  • Your name and student email address
  • Student ID, university, faculty, major, and academic year (where provided)
  • Your profile photo (uploaded by you or provided through Google Sign-In)

Location Information

The Application does not track or monitor your real-time device location.

Weather forecasts are provided based solely on the university campus selected in your profile using the Open-Meteo Weather API. Your physical GPS location is not collected for this feature.

The Application provides AI-powered features using the Google Gemini API. Messages you voluntarily submit to the AI chatbot are processed solely to generate responses. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information while using AI features.


The Service Provider may use your information to provide important service announcements, security notifications, and account-related communications.


For a better experience while using the Application, the Service Provider may require information such as your student email, name, student ID, university, faculty, major, academic year, and profile photo. Account Deletion

You may delete your account directly from within the Application.

After deletion is requested, your account and personal data will be temporarily hidden for 3 days. During this recovery period, you may restore your account at any time. If the account is not restored within 3 days, it will be permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required by law.
